第 2 場Session 22026.09.14

將視覺智慧帶到邊緣，就在 Santa ClaraBringing vision intelligence to the edge, live in Santa Clara.

我們與 Qualcomm Dragonwing 和 EyePop.ai 在 Watch-Me-Build 活動中，以 Innodisk IQ-9075 IQ Studio 逐步展示從韌體燒錄、環境設定到模型下載與即時 AI PoC 驗證的完整部署流程。感謝美商高通國際股份有限公司的邀請。 Together with Qualcomm Dragonwing and EyePop.ai, we showed the full edge deployment journey on Innodisk's IQ-9075 IQ Studio — from firmware flashing and setup to model downloads and live AI PoC validation. Thanks to Qualcomm for the invitation.

第 1 場Session 12026.07.30

Qualcomm AI Hub × YOLO26 BYOM 動手做工作坊Qualcomm AI Hub × YOLO26 BYOM Hands-On Workshop

第一場，早上邀請 Qualcomm、宜鼎、Edge Impulse、Aetina、MoBagel 分享邊緣 AI 趨勢，下午實作把 YOLO26 模型從 FP32 帶到 INT8，部署上 Q911 裝置。 Our first session — morning talks from Qualcomm, Innodisk, Edge Impulse, Aetina, and MoBagel on edge-AI trends, followed by an afternoon hands-on taking a YOLO26 model from FP32 to INT8 and deploying it on the Q911.
